# Site and cable route plan

## Site boundary

The Site comprises Parcels 9, 10 and 11 at Bracken Farm, Wickham, as shown edged red on Drawing SP-01.

## Export cable route

The export cable shall run from the Site to Wickham Primary Substation across Parcels 14 and 15, as shown dashed blue on Drawing SP-02.

## Parcel ownership

Parcel 14 is owned by Mr R. Keane of Fen Lane Farm. Parcel 15 is owned by Wickham Estates Limited.
