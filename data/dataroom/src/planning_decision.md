# Wickham District Council: decision notice

## Application details

Application reference: 24/00318/FUL

Applicant: Brackenfield Solar Ltd. Landowner: Mr J. Hartley, Bracken Farm.

Date of decision: 12 June 2024

## Description of development

Installation of a ground-mounted solar photovoltaic array and battery energy storage system with a maximum export capacity of 49.9MW, together with associated infrastructure.

## Location

Land at Bracken Farm, Wickham, comprising Parcels 9, 10 and 11.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Decision

Wickham District Council hereby grants planning permission for the development described above, subject to the conditions below.

## Condition 1: Time limit

The development hereby permitted shall be begun before the expiration of three years from the date of this permission.

Reason: To comply with section 91 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990.

## Condition 2: Approved plans

The development shall be carried out in accordance with the approved plans listed in Schedule A.

<!-- pagebreak -->

Reason: For the avoidance of doubt and in the interests of proper planning.

## Condition 3: Temporary permission

The permission hereby granted shall be for a limited period of 40 years from the date of First Export, after which the site shall be restored.

Reason: The development is temporary and the land should be returned to agricultural use.

## Condition 4: Decommissioning

Within six months of the cessation of export, a scheme for the removal of the development shall be submitted to the Local Planning Authority for approval.

Reason: To secure the restoration of the site.

## Condition 5: Construction traffic

No development shall commence until a Construction Traffic Management Plan has been submitted to and approved in writing by the Local Planning Authority.

Reason: In the interests of highway safety.
