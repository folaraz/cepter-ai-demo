# Power purchase agreement

## Parties

This Agreement is made between Brackenfield Solar Limited (the "Generator") and Northwind Energy Supply Limited (the "Buyer").

## Schedule 1: Facility

Contracted Capacity: 49.9 MW (AC), measured at the Delivery Point.

The Facility means the solar photovoltaic generating station and co-located battery storage at Bracken Farm, Wickham.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 1. Definitions

"Commercial Operation" means the date on which the Facility first exports electricity under this Agreement.

"Delivery Point" means the Point of Connection to the distribution system at Wickham Primary Substation.

## 2. Term

This Agreement continues for 10 years from Commercial Operation.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Price

The Contract Price shall be £61.50 per MWh, indexed annually to the Consumer Prices Index.

## 4. Delivery

The Buyer shall purchase all electricity exported by the Facility at the Delivery Point.

## 5. Metering

Metering shall comply with the Balancing and Settlement Code.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 6. Commercial Operation

The Generator shall use reasonable endeavours to achieve Commercial Operation on or before 31 March 2029 (the "Target COD").

If Commercial Operation has not occurred by 31 December 2029 (the "Long-Stop Date"), the Buyer may terminate this Agreement by notice to the Generator.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Execution

Signed for and on behalf of Northwind Energy Supply Limited on 3 March 2025.

Signed for and on behalf of Brackenfield Solar Limited on 3 March 2025.
