# Lease of land at Bracken Farm, Wickham

## Parties

This Lease is made on 15 December 2025 between JOHN HARTLEY of Bracken Farm, Wickham (the "Landlord") and Brackenfield Solar Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (the "Tenant").

## Background

The Tenant intends to construct and operate a solar photovoltaic and battery storage facility on the Property.

## 1. The Property

The Property comprises Parcels 9, 10 and 11 as shown edged red on Plan 1 annexed to this Lease.

## 2. Rent

The Tenant shall pay a rent of £1,150 per hectare per annum, payable quarterly in advance.

## 3. Term

The Landlord lets the Property to the Tenant for a term of 35 years from and including 1 January 2026.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Use

The Tenant may use the Property for the generation, storage and export of electricity and for ancillary purposes.

## 5. Rent review

The rent shall be reviewed on every fifth anniversary of the start of the term in line with the Consumer Prices Index.

## 6. Assignment

The Tenant may assign the whole of this Lease with the consent of the Landlord, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 7. Reinstatement

At the end of the term the Tenant shall remove the solar and battery equipment and return the Property to agricultural use within twelve months.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Execution

Signed by John Hartley as the Landlord in the presence of a witness.

Signed for and on behalf of Brackenfield Solar Limited by a director.
