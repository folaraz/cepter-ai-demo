# Offer for connection to the distribution system

## Offer details

Offer reference: FDN/CO/2024/0917

This Connection Offer is made by Fenland Distribution Networks plc to Brackenfield Solar Limited.

## 1. Acceptance

This offer may be accepted by signing and returning it within 90 days of its date of issue, 3 February 2025.

## 2. Site

The Connection is for the generation and storage facility at Bracken Farm, Wickham.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Point of Connection

The Point of Connection shall be the 33 kV busbar at Wickham Primary Substation.

The Customer shall construct the export cable from the Site to the Point of Connection at its own cost.

## 4. Connection

The Maximum Export Capacity at the Point of Connection shall be 45 MW. The Connection Date shall be 1 October 2029, subject to completion of Reinforcement Works at Wickham Primary Substation.

The Maximum Import Capacity at the Point of Connection shall be 20 MW.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 5. Charges

The Connection Charge is £4,850,000, payable in the instalments set out in Schedule 2.

## 6. Execution

Accepted for and on behalf of Brackenfield Solar Limited on 28 March 2025.
